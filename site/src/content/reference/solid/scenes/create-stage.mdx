# createStage

A Stage that runs a three.js scene for its owner: its Host loop, its pointer input and its canvas's size. Under a [`<WardicStage>`](/reference/solid/provider/wardic-stage) the hooks run in its scene. From `@wardic/three/solid`, on Solid 2.

```tsx solid2
import { createStage, WardicStage } from "@wardic/three/solid"
import * as THREE from "three"

const stage = createStage({
  renderer: () => new THREE.WebGLRenderer({ antialias: true }),
  scene,
  camera,
  frameloop: "demand",
})

<WardicStage value={stage}>
  <div class="viewport" ref={stage} />
  <Crate />
</WardicStage>
```

Make it in a component's body. It ends with its owner: its loop, its input and its runtime stop, and the renderer, the scene and the camera stay yours. The [3D guide](/guides/3d) shows it at work.

## Its options

- **`scene`:** the scene it draws, and that a pointer hits.
- **`camera`:** the camera it draws with, or a function giving the one active now.
- **`renderer`:** an instance, in code that runs only in the browser, or a function the browser calls once the page has settled. It's three's `WebGLRenderer` or `WebGPURenderer`, or anything with their `domElement`, `setSize`, `setAnimationLoop` and `render` (`RendererLike`).
- **`frameloop`:** `"always"` draws every frame. `"demand"` draws only when something moves, or after `invalidate()`.
- **`resize`:** `false` leaves the canvas's size to you.
- **`features`:** what it does beyond a ward's motion, `stageDrag` and `stageBatches` ([Stage features](/reference/solid/scenes/stage-features)).
- **The runtime's own options:** `unitScale`, `hitTest` and the rest of [`WardicThree`'s `Options`](/reference/three/wardic-three#options), less its `canvas`, `camera` and `scene`, which the Stage gives it.

## Its Host loop

Once a frame, on `renderer.setAnimationLoop`, in order:
1. the pointer's raycast against what was last drawn;
2. Wardic's frame, its Frame effects after its writes;
3. the render.

Under `frameloop: "demand"`, a frame is drawn when a ward or a Batch moves, a Frame effect asks, or the pointer wants one.

## The Stage it returns

A `ThreeStage`: the `Stage` the hooks find, and its own calls.

- **As a ref,** `ref={stage}`, an element mounts the renderer's canvas and keeps it sized: `setSize`, and a perspective camera's `aspect`.
- **`invalidate()`** draws a frame under `frameloop: "demand"`, for a change Wardic didn't make.
- **`wardic`** is its runtime, for code outside Solid: `stage.wardic.batch(…)`, `stage.wardic.hover(…)`. `undefined` on a server.

## A runtime made outside Solid

`createStage({ wardic, features })` (`AdoptOptions`) adopts a [`WardicThree`](/reference/three/wardic-three) runtime that its maker pumps and draws: a game loop's, an XR session's. The Stage adds no loop of its own. With no view of its own, it drags nothing.

## On a server

A Stage does nothing on a server. Its element renders empty, so hydration matches, and the hooks under it animate nothing. A ward's `read` gives its object's own values.

## Types

- **`StageOptions`**, **`AdoptOptions`:** its two forms' options.
- **`ThreeStage`:** what it returns.
- **`RendererLike`:** what a renderer needs for a Stage to drive it.
- **`isThreeSubject(value)`:** whether a value is one of a scene's Subjects, an `Object3D` or a `Material`, by three's own flags.
