# Solid reference

Every export of Wardic's two Solid bindings, a page each, by family. The two bindings share their names: Solid 1's is `@wardic/solid`, Solid 2's `@wardic/solid/unstable/v2`, and where they differ, a page says how. Hovering a name in your editor shows the short form of its page.

- **[Elements](/reference/solid/elements/w):** `w` and `wardic`, the wards, Solid 2's ward events, and the motion props every one of them takes.
- **[Provider](/reference/solid/provider/wardic-provider):** `WardicProvider`, `WardicConfig`, `LazyWardic`, and the Features.
- **[Presence](/reference/solid/presence/animate-presence):** `AnimatePresence`, and the hooks that hold or hear a leaving child.
- **[Layout](/reference/solid/layout/layout-group):** `LayoutGroup`, `useAnimateLayout`, the instant changes and the projection reset, and the measuring and registering underneath.
- **[Reorder](/reference/solid/reorder/reorder):** `Reorder.Group` and `Reorder.Item`, and the groups and items they're built on.
- **[Gestures](/reference/solid/gestures/create-hover):** a gesture as a signal: hover, press, focus, in view, the page shown, a drag's samples, and drag controls.

The rest of the families follow: live values, imperative animation, transitions and easing, the utilities, and the advanced APIs for building on Wardic.
