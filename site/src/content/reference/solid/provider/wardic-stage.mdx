# WardicStage

The context a scene's Stage is found through. A Stage runs a scene for its owner: its Host loop, its input and its canvas's size. Under one, the hooks run in its scene instead of on the page:

```tsx solid2
import { ward, WardicStage } from "@wardic/solid"

<WardicStage value={stage}>
  <Crate mesh={crate} />
</WardicStage>

function Crate(props: { readonly mesh: Mesh }) {
  const crate = ward(props.mesh, { whileHover: { scale: 1.08 } })
  return crate.held
}
```

- **`ward(subject, config)`** hands the Stage a Subject that's its own, and any accessor of one: the Stage's ward moves it, and has the same events and `read` a ward on an element has.
- **`createFrameEffect`** runs in the Stage's Host loop, after Wardic's writes and before the scene renders.
- **`createHover(mesh)`** and **`createPress(mesh)`** read the pointer over the Subject, and pressed on it, through the Stage's input.
- **An element** stays the page's, under a Stage or not.

## A scene's hooks alone

`ward`, `createBatch` and the sensors take a page's element as well as a scene's object, so each brings the page's half with it. For `ward`, that's the page's ward and every Feature, 57.6 KB gzipped, in an app that may have no page wards at all. Their Stage halves are exported apart, taking only what the Stage above takes:

| Hook | Its Stage half |
| --- | --- |
| `ward(subject, config)` | `wardOnStage` |
| `createBatch({ object \| instances, … })` | `createBatchOnStage` |
| `createHover(subject)` | `createHoverOnStage` |
| `createPress(subject)` | `createPressOnStage` |
| `createDrag(subject)` | `createDragOnStage` |

An Environment's binding re-exports them under the hooks' own names. A scene with no page wards imports them from there, as `@wardic/three/solid`'s `ward`, `createBatch`, `createHover`, `createPress` and `createDrag`, and its app carries none of the page's code: a three.js scene and one ward come to 243.1 KB gzipped, against 300.5 through `@wardic/solid`'s `ward`. A page with both can use either. Given a page's element, each says to import its hook from `@wardic/solid`, in development.

## A ward on a scene's Subject

It takes `initial`, `animate`, `transition`, its own `variants`, `whileHover`, `whileTap` and `custom`. A key of the page's alone (`whileFocus`, `whileInView`) or of an element's (`drag`, `exit`, `layout`, `style`) is an error in development, naming it.

- **`held`** renders nothing. While its Subject or its `animate` is still loading, it holds its component's `<Loading>`, so the fallback shows and `<Reveal>` orders it.
- **Its events** are a ward's: `settled()`, `tapped()`, and `next`, `each` and `when` for `settled`, the hover events and the press events. Each event's `element` is the Subject.
- **`read(key)`** and **`atom(key)`** give the Subject's value for `key` as Wardic draws it. A key the ward hasn't moved reads what the Subject holds, so `ward(light, {}).read("intensity")()` is the light's intensity from the start. A key its Subject has reads at its type: `ward(mesh, {}).read("y")` is an `Accessor<number>`, and `undefined` only while an accessor gives no Subject. A colour reads by its channels in linear light, `colorR`, `colorG` and `colorB`.
- **An accessor Subject:** `ward(() => active(), config)` follows each Subject it gives. A new one takes the ward over, starting from `initial` as the ward did, and the last keeps where it was, with no exit.
- **A key a gesture moves** returns, once the gesture ends, to the value the Subject had before the ward first moved it.
- **The Stage's `animate`** on a Subject a ward is on goes through the ward, which takes the key back at its next change.

`WardicStage` is Solid 2's own provider, `<WardicStage value={stage}>`; read it with `useContext(WardicStage)`, which is `null` outside one. A scene's Subject given to `ward` with no Stage above it is an error that names it, in development.

Scenes come with `@wardic/three/solid`'s `createStage`, which makes a Stage for a three.js scene. The `Stage` interface names no three.js code, so another Environment can fill it: its `ward` is `stageWard(environment)`, given what Wardic's runtime for its Subjects runs on and the scene's input. Solid 2 only.

## Types

- **`Stage`:** what the hooks read of a Stage: whether a value is one of its Subjects, its ward, `animate`, its Frame effects and its pointer input.
- **`StageWard`:** a ward on a Stage's Subject: `held`, `settled`, `next`, `each`, `when` and `read`.
- **`StageSubjects`:** the Subjects a Stage takes, which an Environment adds to by declaration merging: `@wardic/three/solid` adds three.js's objects, so `ward(mesh, config)` is typed.
- **`StageSubject`:** any of them.
- **`StageValues`**, **`StageValueKey`:** the values a Stage's Subjects take beyond an element's, which an Environment adds to by declaration merging: `@wardic/three/solid` adds three.js's, so `ward(mesh, { animate: { roughness: 0.2, emissive: "#ff6a00" } })` typechecks, and a name in neither is still an error naming it.
- **`StageReads`:** what each key of a Stage's Subject reads as, which an Environment adds to by declaration merging, a generic interface of the Subject: `@wardic/three/solid` adds three.js's, each number a scene object has, its material's and its vectors' axes, so `read` gives a mesh's `roughness`, a light's `intensity` or a number of your own on a `Group` as a `number`.
- **`StageRead`**, **`StageReadKey`**, **`StageValueOf`:** a Stage ward's `read`, the keys it reads at its Subject's types, and the type it reads for a key: the Subject's for a key it has, as a page ward's for any other.
- **`StageSubjectOf`:** the Subject a ward is given: itself, or what an accessor gives, `undefined` among it.
- **`StageEvent`**, **`StageEvents`:** the events a ward on a Stage's Subject tells, and each one's info.
- **`stageWard`**, **`StageWardEnvironment`:** a Stage's `ward`, bound to what it runs on: an `animate` environment, where a Subject's keys start (`startLinksOf`) and the scene's `input`.
- **`stageSceneObject`**, **`StageMembership`:** a Scene object's ward, what an Environment's `createSceneObject` builds on. Given what joins its Subject to the scene and what takes it out, it joins once the ward can start, and plays its `exit` before its Subject leaves, past its owner.
- **`StageGraph`:** a scene's graph, which an Environment with one gives its Stage: a Subject's parent, its moves, its place among an ancestor's children, and its descendants. On a Stage with one, a ward with variants and no names of its own follows the nearest ward above its Subject, and its stagger slot is its place among that ward's Subject's children.
- **`StageDrag`**, **`StageDragHandlers`:** what a Stage drags its Subjects with, its Environment's: it watches a Subject's drags on the Stage's input, moves it, and tells each pointer sample and its release coming to rest. A ward's `drag` on a Stage with none is an error in development.
- **`StageDragConfig`**, **`StageDragRange`**, **`StageDragTransition`:** a scene's drag keys, in the page's drag keys' place: `drag` (`true`, or `"x"`, `"y"` or `"z"`), `dragConstraints` per axis as `[min, max]`, `dragElastic`, `dragMomentum`, `dragTransition` (its release's inertia) and `dragSnapToOrigin`. An Environment adds its own by declaration merging: `@wardic/three/solid` adds `dragPlane`.
- **`StageWardConfig`**, **`StageWardConfigValues`**, **`StageWardChecked`:** a ward's config on a Stage's Subject: a ward's, with the scene's drag keys.
- **`StageBatch`**, **`StageKeyedBatchConfig`**, **`StageCountBatchConfig`**, **`StageBatchMembers`**, **`StageBatchValues`**, **`StageBatchTarget`**, **`StageBatchEvents`**, **`StageBatchMemberInfo`:** a scene's Batch, what `createBatch` makes under a Stage given `object` (a three.js object per member, added as it enters and removed once its exit lands) or `instances` (an `InstancedMesh`'s instances), keyed by `each` and `key` or by `count`, with its landings and its members' hovers and presses as a page's Batch's.
- **`StageBatchBinding`**, **`StageBatchHooks`:** what a Stage's `batch` gives `createBatch`, and what it tells it: an Environment's side of a scene's Batch.
- **`SceneWard`**, **`SceneObjectEvent`**, **`SceneObjectEvents`**, **`SceneObjectExit`:** a Scene object's handle, a ward that also tells `exitStart` and `exitComplete`, and their info: the Subject leaving, as `element`.
