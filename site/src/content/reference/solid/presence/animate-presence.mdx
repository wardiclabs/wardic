# AnimatePresence

Wraps children that come and go, a `<Show>`'s or a `<For>`'s, and keeps each one that leaves on the page until its `exit` has played: its Wardic elements' exits, inner ones included. See [Exit animations](/guides/presence).

```tsx
import { AnimatePresence, w } from "@wardic/solid" // or "@wardic/solid/unstable/v2"

<AnimatePresence>
  <Show when={open()}>
    <w.div initial={{ opacity: 0 }} animate={{ opacity: 1 }} exit={{ opacity: 0 }} />
  </Show>
</AnimatePresence>
```

A child that swaps for another needs to be told apart: `<Show keyed>`, or a `key` on its root element. One that comes back while one with its key is still leaving takes over from it, from where it's drawn.

## Props

| Prop | |
| --- | --- |
| `mode` | How a child enters while another leaves: `"sync"` (the default), at once, beside it; `"wait"`, once every leaver has gone; `"popLayout"`, at once, the leaver taken out of the flow, so the children after it move into its place; an `<svg>` root is popped too, beyond Motion's (under a rotated or skewed ancestor, off its place), while the elements inside an `<svg>` leave where they are. |
| `initial` | `false`: the child present at first render starts at its `animate`, with no entrance. Later ones enter as usual. |
| `custom` | What a leaving child's function `exit` is given, over its own `custom`, which can't change once it has left. |
| `anchorX`, `anchorY` | Where `popLayout` pins a leaver: by its `"left"` (the default) or `"right"`, and its `"top"` (the default) or `"bottom"`. |
| `presenceAffectsLayout` | `false`: children coming and going don't move the `layout` elements around them. Default `true`. |
| `propagate` | Inside another presence's child: when that child leaves, this presence's children leave too, each playing its exit. |
| `onExitComplete` | Solid 1: called once every leaving child has gone. |
| `ref` | Solid 2: a [presence handle](/reference/solid/presence/create-presence), which tells the same, and more. |

## Coming from Motion

Motion's `AnimatePresence`, with its props. Motion tells its children apart by React's `key`; here it's `<Show keyed>`, or the `key` prop on a Wardic element.

## Types

- **`AnimatePresenceProps`:** its props, above.
- **`PresenceMode`:** `mode`'s values.
