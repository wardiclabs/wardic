# Examples

Wardic Northlight is a made-up ceramics shop whose every feature is an example. Each page has the feature to use, why it's worth reaching for, what reduced motion does to it, and its code. The shop runs on [Solid 2](https://solid2.wardic.dev) and on [Foldkit](https://foldkit.wardic.dev), each its own app with its own code; most examples are on both.

Each guide links its own examples at its end, under "In the storefront".
