# Roadmap

What comes next, in the order it's planned. The order is the plan, and this page gives no dates for what's ahead; what has shipped says when. Each milestone and its issues are on GitHub, where you can follow them.

The 1.x milestones number `@wardic/core`, `@wardic/dom` and `@wardic/test`, which version together, `@wardic/foldkit` from 1.2, and `@wardic/object` and `@wardic/three` from 1.3. `@wardic/solid` versions on its own since its 2.0, depending on the exact `@wardic/core` and `@wardic/dom` it's built with.

## 1.0: Motion's API on Solid

Wardic 1.0 gives Solid the components, props and hooks of Motion for React, on Solid 1 and on Solid 2. From `1.0.0`, its stable entries follow semver: `@wardic/solid`'s root, Solid 1's binding until 2.0, and `@wardic/dom`, `@wardic/core` and `@wardic/test`. `@wardic/core`'s and `@wardic/dom`'s `unstable/` modules are for Wardic's own packages and binding authors, outside semver: they can change in any release. The Solid 2 binding was `@wardic/solid/unstable/v2`, outside semver until Solid 2 was stable; [`@wardic/solid` 2.0](#solid-2-by-default) made it the root. The Effect-typed parts, `@wardic/core`, `@wardic/dom`, `@wardic/test`, the provider's `env` and the binding entries whose pages say so, use Effect's own types, so they move to a new Effect major only in a Wardic major.

Beyond Motion's API, 1.0 brings `createBatch`: hundreds or thousands of like elements, a grid's tiles or a chart's points, animated as one run, so changing all their targets costs about a twentieth of changing them one at a time ([#665](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/665), [Many elements at once](/guides/batches)).

[The 1.0.0 milestone](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/milestone/1): [#533](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/533), [#554](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/554), [#555](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/555), [#556](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/556), [#557](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/557), [#558](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/558), [#559](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/559), [#560](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/560), [#665](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/665)

## 1.1: size and speed

Faster, and smaller only where it costs no speed. The size work 1.0 left (#518) landed before it: the Slim element's app went from 104 to 97 KB gzipped on Solid 1, and from 132 to 117 on Solid 2. #746 took it to 94 and 115, with every app smaller, and `bun run size`'s budgets hold it there. What it brings:

- **Scroll on the compositor:** scroll-linked values handed to the browser's own scroll-driven animations (`ScrollTimeline`), as Motion hands some, so they don't trail the scroll by a frame.
- **Wardic instances:** `createWardic(features)` returns the elements and hooks those Features allow, so the type checker knows what each element can do, and an element of another Feature's is an error that names it.

[The 1.1 milestone](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/milestone/2): [#240](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/240), [#516](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/516)

## Solid 2 by default

Solid 2 is stable, so `@wardic/solid` 2.0 makes its binding the root, under semver, its API as `@wardic/solid/unstable/v2` had it. Solid 1's binding moves to `@wardic/solid/v1`: a Solid 1 app changes its imports from `@wardic/solid` to `@wardic/solid/v1` (see [Start on Solid 1](/start/solid-1)). `/v1` is deprecated and frozen for features, and `@wardic/solid/unstable/v2` stays as a deprecated alias of the root; both go at Wardic's next major, when `@wardic/core` takes its 2.0 and `@wardic/solid` its next major beside it. These docs are one site for both bindings, Solid 2's first.

Only `@wardic/solid` takes the major: `@wardic/core`, `@wardic/dom` and `@wardic/test` stay on 1.x, and the milestones below go on from 1.2.

[The 2.0 milestone](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/milestone/7): [#566](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/566)

## 1.2: Foldkit

A Wardic binding for [Foldkit](https://www.npmjs.com/package/foldkit), the Effect-native UI framework. `@wardic/dom` was designed to serve it beside the Solid bindings.

`@wardic/foldkit` shipped as 1.2.0 in November 2026 ([#795](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/795)): [Start on Foldkit](/start/foldkit), the [Foldkit reference](/reference/foldkit), and the storefront on Foldkit at [foldkit.wardic.dev](https://foldkit.wardic.dev), every example's code beside it.

[The 1.2 milestone](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/milestone/3): [#561](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/561)

## 1.3: 3D

Three.js scenes, animated with Wardic's springs, variants and gestures. You make your objects however you like, in code, from a loader or through another library, and Wardic animates them: it has no scene components and no renderer of its own. Its writes land in the frame your scene's loop draws, never a frame late.

`@wardic/object` and `@wardic/three` shipped as 1.3.0 in November 2026 ([#564](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/564)), with `@wardic/solid` 2.1.0 for their Solid 2 hooks:

- **Any object's numeric properties,** whatever draws them, as Motion's `animate(object, …)` does: `@wardic/object`, the base for canvas and WebGL ([its reference](/reference/object)).
- **Three.js,** in `@wardic/three` ([its reference](/reference/three)):
  - `x`, `rotateY` and `scale` keys, as on the page;
  - a material's, a light's and a camera's own numbers;
  - colours blended in linear light;
  - a pointer's hover and press, found by raycasting the whole scene.
- **Many objects as one Batch,** an `InstancedMesh`'s instances among them, as `createBatch` moves many elements on the page. Ten thousand instances cost 0.64 ms a frame.
- **Hooks for Solid 2,** in `@wardic/three/solid`, with no component to add, and none of the page's code:
  - `createStage` runs the canvas and its loop;
  - `ward(mesh, config)` gives an object a `w.div`'s targets, variants and transitions, and reads its keys at their types;
  - `createHover`, `createPress` and `createDrag` hear the pointer through the scene's raycast;
  - `createSceneObject` adds an object to the scene and plays its exit before it leaves;
  - `createFrameEffect` runs your own code in the frame, before it's drawn.

The [3D guide](/guides/3d) shows each, with live scenes, and its hooks are in the Solid reference's Scenes pages, beginning with [`createStage`](/reference/solid/scenes/create-stage). The storefront has four scenes, live at [solid2.wardic.dev](https://solid2.wardic.dev), each with its code:
- [turn the vase](https://solid2.wardic.dev/scenes/turn-the-vase);
- [kintsugi](https://solid2.wardic.dev/scenes/kintsugi), 3,000 shards in one Batch;
- [the shelf](https://solid2.wardic.dev/scenes/the-shelf);
- [fire the kiln](https://solid2.wardic.dev/scenes/fire-the-kiln).

[The 1.3 milestone](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/milestone/8): [#564](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/564), [#881](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/881), [#882](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/882), [#883](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/883), [#884](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/884), [#885](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/885), [#927](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/927), [#934](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/934), [#935](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/935), [#936](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/936), [#937](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/937), [#939](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/939)

## 1.4: a performance viewer

A panel in the page, in development only, so you can see what each animation costs: every running animation, the element it's on, and its cost each frame, in JavaScript time, style writes and layout reads, from the spans Wardic already emits. A dropped or long frame shows which animations ran in it.

[The 1.4 milestone](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/milestone/4): [#562](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/562)

## 1.5: Timeline

A Timeline you can scrub, in the style of GSAP, for choreography that Motion's sequences can't nest, reverse or seek: animations placed at times or at labels, Timelines inside Timelines, and the whole played, paused, sought, reversed and sped up as one, or scrubbed by the scroll or any value. It builds on core's Sequence, which matches Motion's sequences today.

[The 1.5 milestone](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/milestone/5): [#563](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/563)

## 1.6: beyond the DOM

More Environments beyond the browser's DOM, after 3D:

- **The terminal,** through [OpenTUI](https://github.com/sst/opentui)'s Solid renderer.
- **Layout animations there,** which need the Projection system moved into core, behind a service each Environment provides.

[The 1.6 milestone](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/milestone/6): [#565](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/565), [#16](https://github.com/wardiclabs/wardic/issues/16)

## Not planned

Bindings for React, Vue or web components: Motion already serves React, Vue and plain JavaScript.
