A server-rendered Batch draws each spread member's start, so a hydrated page doesn't jump: its `initial`'s values, its targets with `initial: false` or, keyed, under `<AnimatePresence initial={false}>`, else nothing, as a Wardic element's, over the element's own `style` given to its handle. A keyed Batch reads its list once on the server. The server checks what the client does, with its messages. `DomBatch.styleOf(values)` gives the `transform` and `opacity` the Batch writer draws.
