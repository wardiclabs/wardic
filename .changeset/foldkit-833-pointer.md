`Wardic.pointer(element, options?)`: the pointer over an element as Live values, on the Handle's registry (#833): its place in the element's pixels, its progress across the element's box from 0 to 1 each way, and whether it's inside, each an Atom a view binds, set within the pointer's own events and held while the caller's Scope lives; a mouse and a pen unless `types` says. Plain pointer events, not the hover gesture: no Feature to name, nothing to fail.
