A Batch's entrance can play from the first paint of a server-rendered page: `appear: true` on `createBatch` (both forms) or on `Reorder.BatchGroup` writes one CSS animation for its members on one set of transitions, each member's own values on its element, and hydration takes each member over where its CSS has got to, at its speed there. Off by default: a Batch that doesn't ask renders its start, as before. A `Reorder.BatchGroup` item's `initial` keys other than `x` and `y` now move to their identity as it enters, so `initial={{ opacity: 0 }}` fades it in; before, it held there.
