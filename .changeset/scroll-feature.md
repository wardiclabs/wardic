A container's scroll leaves the Probe: core's `ScrollSource` service holds it, with its types: `ScrollSource.Options`, `Scroll` and `Axis`, which were `Probe.ScrollOptions`, `Scroll` and `ScrollAxis`, the DOM's is `DomScrollSource.layer`, and `@wardic/test`'s Probe layers give one beside the Probe. `LiveValue.scroll` needs a `ScrollSource`. In the Solid bindings, the new `scroll` Feature builds it, the Environment's when it gives one, else the DOM's, and `createScroll` brings it itself, so app code doesn't change and an app that reads no scroll ships none of its code: about 1.7 KB gzipped less.
