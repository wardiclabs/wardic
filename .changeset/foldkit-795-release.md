`@wardic/foldkit`, Wardic's binding for [Foldkit](https://www.npmjs.com/package/foldkit), ships (#561, ADR-0071): Motion's animations on Foldkit's views, as Effect. One Handle a program, `Wardic.make({ env, features })`, whose `html(h)` gives a view the `w.*` elements, with Motion's props as attributes: `w.Initial`, `w.Animate`, `w.Transition` and `w.Variants`, the gestures (`w.WhileHover`, `w.WhileTap`, `w.WhileFocus`, `w.WhileInView`), `w.Drag` and its options, `w.Layout`, `w.LayoutId` and `w.LayoutGroup`, `w.Presence` and `w.Exit`, `w.ReorderGroup` and `w.ReorderItem`; Messages where Motion has callbacks; Atoms for the Live values (`w.Style`, `w.Text`, `w.OnUpdate`, `Wardic.scroll`, `Wardic.pointer`); `Wardic.animate` and `Wardic.animateLayout` as Effects for Commands, with Animation controls, Animation scopes and Drag controls made apart from any element; the Features a Handle names or loads; server rendering through Foldkit's `renderToString`, the Appear animations playing from the first paint and `wardic.hydrate` taking them over; and Scene tests resolving its Mounts by name. Its peer is Foldkit `~0.170.0 || ~0.171.0`. See [Start on Foldkit](https://wardic.dev/start/foldkit) and the [Foldkit reference](https://wardic.dev/reference/foldkit).
