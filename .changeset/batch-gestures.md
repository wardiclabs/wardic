A Batch's members hover and press: `createBatch`'s `whileHover` and `whileTap` take values with a `transition`, or a function of a member's index (keyed: its item and index), each member's layered over `animate`'s targets as a Wardic element's Override stack is, a Batch change mid-gesture moving only the keys the gesture doesn't hold. Enter and Space press a focused member, whose element takes `tabindex="0"`. Solid 1 has `onHoverStart`, `onHoverEnd`, `onTapStart`, `onTap` and `onTapCancel`; Solid 2 `next` and `when` of `hoverStart`, `hoverEnd`, `pressStart`, `tap` and `tapCancel`, each with the member's `i` and `item`. A Batch listens from one source each, its gesture code loaded at its first need. Core's `InputSource` gains optional `pointerAmong` and `focusAmong` feeds over many targets, which the DOM's input gives, and `Recognizer.watchHoverAmong` and `watchPressAmong` watch them; `TestInput.pushable`'s `pushTo` and `pushFocusTo` feed them.
